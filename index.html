Technique · Prioritise — the Eisenhower Matrix

Only two things can start today

Tasks 1 and 2 are the only ones in Do now. Everything else is waiting on something, so it goes in Schedule. That is the whole lesson of this step: priority follows dependency.

  • Task 1, the room. Nothing waits on it, but almost everything else does. Book it first.
  • Task 5 is the interesting one. Nothing blocks it, so you could do it today — but nothing needs it until the invitation on day 8. Unblocked is not the same as urgent.
  • The agenda comes before the presenters. People should be invited to an approved programme, not a draft.
  • Task 7 is the only one that waits on three things — task 1 the room, task 5 the email addresses, task 6 the finalised agenda. An invitation needs a venue, the right addresses and a programme. Naming any one of the three is a fair answer, but it cannot go out until all three are done.
  • Nothing lands in Delegate or Drop. That was step 2: the tasks you did not tick were the Drop box.
  • Watch the lead time notes. That is time somebody else needs, and it is not the same as how long the task takes you.

Technique · Schedule — time blocking

Working days

One workable answer

There is more than one right plan. Any order that respects the dependencies and the lead times works.

  • Four tasks have waiting time built in — the HR Director takes a day, the presenters take two, the speakers take two to send files, and invitees need five days to reply. That waiting is why the invitation goes out on day 5 and not day 8.
  • Nothing new starts on days 7 to 9. You are waiting for replies. Waiting time is not spare time.
  • Days 12 and 13 are the genuine slack. Spare time is not wasted time — it is what absorbs the thing that goes wrong.
  • Catering is the trap. Nothing waits on it, so it feels safe to leave late. But the supplier needs three days, so day 12 is the last possible day.
  • Focus — Pomodoro. When you block 9:00 to 9:30 on day 1 for the room, that block is one 25-minute round. One task, timer on, email closed.
